My approach to a product change

Milestone-based PLM approach, tracked in ENOVIA in seven phases: the change request (ECR) is investigated, designed and validated, then the change order (ECO) and the Industrial Release Order (RO) release the PDEF and PREA parts for series production. Each phase ends with a review (go / no-go). Click a phase.

PLM glossary

ECR

Engineering Change Request: the change request, investigated and validated before decision.

ECO

Engineering Change Order: the order that applies the decision.

RO

Industrial Release Order: releases the parts for series production.

PDEF

Part definition: the designed technical reference (drawings, requirements).

PREA

Part realization: a supplier’s realization linked to a definition; one PDEF can have several PREA.

APQP and PPAP: securing industrialization after the ECR decision

Once the change is approved, the supplier runs it through the APQP approach, then proves it with a PPAP file. I follow each phase and review the documents before approval. AIAG framework, presented generically.

The 5 APQP phases

1Plan and define

Needs, quality and reliability targets, preliminary list of special characteristics.

2Product design and development

Design FMEA (DFMEA), design reviews, drawings, DVP&R validation plan.

3Process design and development

Process flow, process FMEA (PFMEA), pre-launch control plan, work instructions.

4Product and process validation

Significant production run (run at rate), capability studies, MSA, validation tests, PPAP submission.

5Feedback and corrective action

Series follow-up, variation reduction, lessons learned and continuous improvement.

The 18 PPAP elements
  1. 01Design records (drawings)
  2. 02Authorized engineering changes
  3. 03Customer engineering approval
  4. 04Design FMEA (DFMEA)
  5. 05Process flow diagram
  6. 06Process FMEA (PFMEA)
  7. 07Control plan
  8. 08Measurement system analysis (MSA)
  9. 09Dimensional results
  10. 10Material and performance results
  11. 11Initial process capability studies
  12. 12Qualified laboratory documentation
  13. 13Appearance approval report
  14. 14Sample production parts
  15. 15Master sample
  16. 16Checking aids
  17. 17Customer-specific requirements
  18. 18Part submission warrant (PSW)

💡 The submission level (1 to 5) depends on the risk of the change: from the warrant alone (PSW) to the full file reviewed on site.
